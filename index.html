Our Story

Founded in 2024

Technet Technologies was officially founded in 2024 at the St Anthony's High School dormitories. The vision began while the founders were rigorously studying Maths, Physics, and Computer Science, turning their shared passion for technology into a global enterprise.

We owe a huge debt of gratitude to our inspiring teachers who shaped our foundation: Mr. Masvaya T (Computer Science), Mr. Mutero (Physics), and Mr. Nzvimba (Pure Mathematics).
